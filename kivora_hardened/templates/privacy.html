Privacy Policy

Your data, on your terms

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

We store the data you give us, tied to your account:

  • Account details — name, email, and a securely hashed password.
  • Journal entries — the text you write, plus the detected mood, sentiment, and crisis flag.
  • Assessment results — your check-in inputs and the model outputs (risk flag, wellbeing score).
  • Behavioral data — habit check-ins, mind-game scores, focus sessions, recovery-plan progress, and student-mode entries.
  • Memory — short "facts" extracted from your journal (e.g. "stressed about exams"), which we use to notice cross-time patterns.
  • Feedback & testimonials — anything you submit through the Feedback pages.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your journal text or predictions with advertisers or third parties.

How your data is used

Your data powers the app itself: mood trends, risk insights, personalized recovery plans, and the companion. Where an AI model (e.g. Google Gemini) is enabled, only the specific, bounded context needed for a single request is sent to it (for example, one journal entry, or the structured memory facts for a pattern insight) — never your whole account, and never other users' data. When no AI model is configured, everything runs locally on deterministic rules and your data never leaves our servers.

Memory you can see and delete

The Memory page shows every fact the app has learned about you. You can delete individual facts, or clear all of your memory (including period summaries) at any time. Deleting a fact removes it from future insights and memory-based responses immediately.

Your controls

  • Download your data. Settings → "Download my data" gives you a complete JSON copy of everything tied to your account.
  • Delete your account. Settings → "Delete account" permanently removes your account and all associated data. Type DELETE to confirm; this cannot be undone.
  • Consent. You confirmed the Privacy Policy when you signed up. We record the date of that consent.

What we do not claim

Kivora is a self-tracking and self-help tool. Its predictions are statistical estimates from models, not medical or psychological diagnoses, and we say so wherever results are shown. If you're in crisis, contact local emergency services or a qualified mental-health professional.

Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use HTTP-only cookies with SameSite protection, and every state-changing request is protected against cross-site request forgery. In production the app refuses to start without a proper signing secret.

Questions

Use the Feedback page if you're already signed in, or contact us from the sign-up page. We'll respond within 5 business days.
